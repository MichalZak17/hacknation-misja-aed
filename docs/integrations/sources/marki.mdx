## Mapping to reference fields

Same as [Krosno](./krosno.mdx), after unwrapping the outer CSV.

## Quirks

- **Every line is wrapped in quotes,** including the header, with the inner quotes doubled. The connector unwraps it
  before parsing.
- **Coordinates are swapped:** the header says longitude first, but the values are latitude first. Validation swaps
  them back and flags `coordinates_swapped`.
- **Row 7 is a copy-paste error:** it holds Krosno's coordinates (`21.7203…, 49.7188…`) from the template. It is
  rejected as `outside_municipality`.
- **Rows 5 and 6 share coordinates** (`shared_coordinates`). Names have trailing spaces, and there are typos such as
  "Al..".
